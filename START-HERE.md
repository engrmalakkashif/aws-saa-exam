## 💡 Top Tips for Success

### ✅ Do These Things
- Study a little every day (consistency > intensity)
- Take hands-on labs on AWS Free Tier
- Take multiple practice exams
- Focus on weak areas
- Sleep well before the exam

- Only memorizing (understand concepts)
- Skipping practice questions
- Focusing on just one domain
- Stressing excessively
